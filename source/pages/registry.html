A model and benchmark pair that more than one source reports is the only place where published scores can be checked against each other. For each pair the registry answers two questions from the records alone: were the scores produced the same way, and are they independent measurements? Most of the time the records do not say.

Produced the same way? Independent?

Spread is the distance between the highest and the lowest score, in percentage points, for scores on a 0 to 1 or 0 to 100 scale. Pairs are matched by normalized names, as in the paper: a missed match hides a pair, and a wrong merge would create a disagreement that is not there.
